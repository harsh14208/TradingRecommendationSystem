What We Measure

  • Phantom-win corrected: Every signal where the stop was hit intraday is scored as a loss, even if price recovered by the 7-day mark. This is the honest broker-fill number.
  • Net-of-friction: Returns include a 0.50% round-trip friction assumption — conservative for large-cap but deliberate, to avoid overstating edge.
  • Brier score: How well-calibrated our confidence numbers are. 0.25 = random guessing. 0.264 = slight skill, but still ~11pp overconfident.
  • Concurrent drawdown: We simulate 5 concurrent positions to measure realistic portfolio risk. Per-trade MaxDD (−0.85%) is 8× too optimistic.
